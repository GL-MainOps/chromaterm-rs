1. Log levels and status words
------------------------------
Severity escalates from calm to loud. The worst levels get a red background:
FATAL: disk array lost, PANIC in kernel, CRITICAL temperature, CRIT sensor,
EMERG power, EMERGENCY shutdown and ALERT raised by the watchdog.
Errors are bold red: ERROR, Error and error, err, errors, exception,
Traceback, fail, fails, failed, failure, failures, denied, refused, rejected,
unreachable, unauthorized, forbidden, invalid, aborted, segfault, panicked,
timeout, timed out and time out.
Warnings are amber: WARN, warn, warning, warnings, WARNING, deprecated,
degraded and retrying.
Success words are green: ok, OK, okay, success, successful, successfully,
succeeded, passed, healthy, ready, running, active, enabled, accepted,
connected, established, listening, complete and completed.
Informational levels are blue or teal: INFO and NOTICE. Quiet levels are gray:
DEBUG, TRACE and VERBOSE.

10. Control: near-misses
------------------------
These lines must stay completely plain (word boundaries protect them):
The errand runner was invalidated by okapis in okinawa.
Word boundaries: terror, warnings_count_x, FATALITY, statusok, alerting.
Not a MAC address: aa:bb:cc. Not an email: user@localhost.

Known look-alikes (colored on purpose, so you know what to expect):
999.999.999.999 is not a valid IP, so it is NOT colored as an IP; the first
three groups still read as a version number. 1.2.3 is a valid version. In
abc1.2.3def the lone 2 between dots is colored as a number.
